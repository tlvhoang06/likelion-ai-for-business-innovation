Business Problem · The 10-Day Regulatory Window

A typhoon stops farm income.
The 10-day overdue clock still runs.

10days overdue

To legally extend a disaster-hit farm loan and keep it in Debt Group 1 · Performing Loan, bank restructuring must assess eligibility on the borrower's request while the loan is within term or up to 10 days overdue (≤10d, Group 1).

Cir. 29/2025/TT-NHNN, Art. 2, 3 & 8 · Dec. 55/2015/NĐ-CP & Dec. 156/2025/NĐ-CP · Cir. 31/2024/TT-NHNN.

Why manual paper dossiers miss the 10-day window

After a storm, roads flood and power fails—yet each farmer must travel to their commune office for a separate paper damage certificate before the bank can review relief.

What happens past 10 days overdue?
5%Group 2 provision
20%Group 3 provision

Automatic downgrade & mandatory bank risk reserve

Dec. 86/2024/NĐ-CP · mandatory specific risk provision locked from bank capital.

If restructuring is not completed within ≤10 days overdue, the loan shifts from Group 1 · 0% specific provision to Group 2 · Attention-Needed (not yet bad debt), requiring banks to book a 5% potential specific risk provision and restricting farmers' access to new replanting loans.

Core business problem: Banks and farmers must verify storm damage and process relief before the loan reaches 10 days overdue—using a paper workflow that takes weeks.
Sources: Cir. 29/2025/TT-NHNN, Art. 2, 3 & 8; Dec. 86/2024/NĐ-CP; Cir. 31/2024/TT-NHNN.

Early Alert Tracks
& Blind-Spot Rank

  • Priority (R1|R2): 1,338 communes (>200mm rain or river ≥AL3 [≥BĐ3] across 6 basins).
  • Watchlist (R3): 1,354 wind-only communes queued for SAR confirmation.
  • 0–100 Triage Score: Auditable heuristic ranking 355 out-of-scan pockets.

Layer 2 · Live Stage-2 ML + Gemini Copilot

Post-Event ML Gate
& Tool-Grounded Triage

Stage-2 Supervised ML (4-Fold CV)

HistGradientBoosting fuses post-event S1 (08–20 Sep) + R2 + R1 + HAND ≤10m across 2,540 classifiable communes (13/11 CV: 63.46% Prec / 79.11% Rec; 215-R2 re-run: 63.38%/79.81%)—catching 256/321 Watchlist & 52/63 missed floods; keeps 355 Unknown.

Gemini Zero-PII Function Calling

Runs 4 declared tools (query_communes, plan_branch_triage, get_river_stations, dispatch_ui_action) over GID_3 + s2_ml_prob only—zero PII; explicit Offline Fallback mode when no key is set.

Layer 3 · Phase 2 Pilot Calibration

Multi-Storm ML
& Multimodal Intake

Extend single-storm CV to cross-event validation using anonymized multi-season bank Group 2+ records, GloFAS hourly river flows, and paper/photo intake OCR.

Honest ML & Timing Scope

Stage-2 ML uses post-event SAR (08–20 Sep, pre-21 Sep composite) + intra-Yagi provincial CV—so it gates post-event confirmation only and leaves <72h turnaround (post-SAR to ready) as a pilot SLA target.

Strict Human-in-the-Loop Guardrail

Neither Stage-2 ML nor Gemini auto-approves or denies loans. AI triages exceptions, groups district capacity lists (not route optimization), and drafts memos—banks retain 100% approval authority.
